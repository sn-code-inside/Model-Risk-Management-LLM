========================================================================
03_regulatory_scoping — one inventory, per-use-case classification
========================================================================

Retail credit decisioning assistant  (EU, UK)
    - PRA SS1/23
        UK PRA-regulated bank: model within Principle 1 definition; principles apply regardless of the US carve-out
    - EU AI Act — Annex III high-risk (5(b))
        Creditworthiness scoring of natural persons; high-risk obligations apply from 2 Dec 2027 (Digital Omnibus)
    - FCA Consumer Duty (PS22/9)
        Retail customers: four-outcomes evidence attaches to the use case
    - GDPR/UK GDPR Article 22
        Automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect; UK position as amended by DUAA 2025
    - Equality Act 2010 s.19
        Indirect-discrimination exposure through proxy features
    - SM&CR
        Named senior-manager accountability for the model risk framework

AML alert triage  (US)
    - SR 26-2 (principles-by-analogy)
        Generative/agentic AI is outside SR 26-2's formal scope; the letter expects the underlying principles to be applied
    - BSA/AML supervisory expectations
        SR 21-8 rescinded into SR 26-2; AML models covered by the general framework

Internal IT support chatbot  (UK)
    - PRA SS1/23
        UK PRA-regulated bank: model within Principle 1 definition; principles apply regardless of the US carve-out
    - SM&CR
        Named senior-manager accountability for the model risk framework

Insurance underwriting assistant  (EU)
    - EU AI Act — Annex III high-risk (5(c))
        Life/health insurance pricing high-risk category

Four use cases, four different regulatory scopes — a blanket rating cannot capture this.
